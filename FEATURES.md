# WrestleDesk features

Working name. Built for an owner-coach who will use it at their own club first: independent wrestling, a youth program, or a small BJJ / MMA room. The three jobs that matter most are memberships, sending the bill, and sending and tracking waivers. Everything else is on the desk, but thinner.

Example software pricing on the marketing page ($49 / $89 / $149) is not a quote.

## Must-have (MVP)

1. **Household memberships.** The bill sits on the parent, not on each athlete. One household can hold two or three kids. Show the single-dues total, the family price, and the sibling discount as plain math.
2. **Plans a small club actually sells.** Youth, high school, adult wrestling, BJJ gi, gi + no-gi, family of two, family of three, and a 10-visit punch for drop-ins.
3. **Monthly billing run with a preview.** Choose who is not on autopay, plus anyone already past due. Optional mat fee lines for tournament sign-ups. Read the invoice and the email or text. Then queue the run. Nothing sends until that confirm step.
4. **Invoice list.** Draft, sent, paid, failed, past due. A coach can mark an invoice paid at the desk when someone hands them cash. That mark is a note, not a card capture.
5. **Delinquency queue.** Order of operations: reminder, retry, pause mat access. Each step is recorded with a time. Paused athletes show a flag at check-in.
6. **Front-desk check-in.** Search or 4-digit PIN. Roster for the class on the mat, with unpaid and missing-waiver flags. Checking in updates the count. A punch card loses a visit.
7. **Weekly schedule.** Youth, high school, adult, gi, no-gi, open mat, with the coach and the room on each block.
8. **Waivers.** One template. Per athlete: not sent, sent, opened, signed, expired, and expiring soon. Send to picked athletes or a whole household by email or SMS link. Resend. Opening the parent link moves sent → opened. Signing on the parent page moves it to signed for a year.
9. **Coach roster.** USA Wrestling coach card, SafeSport, background check, first aid. Expiry on the same desk clock as the rest of the demo.
10. **Parent page.** Kid schedule, recent attendance, balance, a pay button that does not charge anything, waiver signing, tournament sign-up (mat fee can ride the next billing run).
11. **Demo boundary.** Sample club only. Data stays in the browser. No payment-processor keys and no calls to one.

## Later

- Live card processing and real autopay retries (the desk marks a retry today; a processor would run it).
- Real email and SMS delivery, with a delivered / failed receipt. The demo only queues the message.
- USA Wrestling card lookup for athletes, not just a number stored on the member.
- Trackwrestling (or whatever the state uses) for entries. Mat fees can be billed here; registration stays on the tournament site until that exists.
- Weigh-in history and the weight class they actually made, not just the one on the roster.
- Skin-infection hold so check-in can refuse a kid without the coach having to remember.
- Class caps and a waitlist for the 8U room when it fills.
- Belt promotions and promotion fees for the BJJ side.
- Private lessons on the calendar.
- Gear and singlet charges on the same household bill.
- Multiple coaches with their own logins, and a locked check-in kiosk.
- More than one club location.
- QuickBooks export and sales tax if a state requires it on dues.
- Lead box for trial weeks, then a one-click convert to a youth plan (Ellis Walsh is the sample trial).
- Parent push or a real text thread, instead of the demo link.
- Volunteer hours so tournament staffing is not another spreadsheet.
- 1099s if the club pays assistant coaches.

Punch passes, drop-ins, mat fees, and USA Wrestling card dates are already visible in the sample so the later work has somewhere to land. They are not the whole product yet.
