How billing works

One family, one number, then a chase if it fails

  1. 01
    The plan lives on the household.

    Youth, high school, adult, gi, no-gi, or a family price. A 10-visit punch sits beside them for the wrestler who is only in town a month.

  2. 02
    Once a month, preview the run.

    See every line, including the sibling discount, before anyone gets a message. Leave autopay households off the list.

  3. 03
    Send email or a text.

    The parent page shows the balance and a pay button. On this demo the button stops in the browser. No Stripe keys. No card network.

  4. 04
    If it fails: remind, retry, pause.

    The queue is the order Dana already uses. Mark each step. Pausing access flags them at check-in. The kid can still be let on the mat — that call stays with the coach.

FAQ

Before you ask at the desk

Does this charge a parent's card?

No. The pay form and the “save card” form clear themselves. They do not store a number and they do not call a payment processor. Mark paid, on the invoice list, is a desk note.

Is the pricing real?

The $49 / $89 / $149 tiers are labeled example pricing. Athlete dues inside the sample club — $89 youth, $199 for three kids — are also fake numbers for Lot 9 Wrestling in Des Moines.

Can I bill a whole family at once?

Yes. Open Alvarez. Three athletes, one payer, sibling discount spelled out. Then run November billing and preview that invoice before you queue it.

What if they don't pay?

Brennan is past due. Nguyen's card failed. The queue lets you mark a reminder, a retry, then pause mat access. Santos is already paused so you can see the end of that path.

How do waivers get signed?

From the waiver desk, select athletes and queue an email or a text. Status moves through sent, opened, signed, and expired. On the parent page, sign Sofia and the desk shows signed.

USA Wrestling cards?

Coach cards, SafeSport, and background checks are on the coach roster with expiry. Athlete card numbers sit on the member. A live USAW lookup is later.

Tournaments and mat fees?

Parents can put a kid on Iowa Kids, the girls duals, or the BJJ open. The billing run can add the mat fee. You still register on the tournament site. This demo does not connect to one.

Where does the data live?

In this browser only. Reset sample data is at the bottom of the desk sidebar. Refreshing keeps your clicks. Another browser starts clean.
